W WriteFix for Outlook

Adds a WriteFix button to Outlook while you write an email. It fixes spelling and grammar, or improves the wording, using your own Claude or OpenAI API key, and shows every change before it's made. Works in new Outlook and classic Outlook for Windows, Outlook for Mac, and Outlook on the web.

Install it (once, for all your computers)

  1. Open aka.ms/olksideload and sign in with your work account. Outlook on the web opens a box called Add-ins for Outlook.
  2. On the left, click My add-ins.
  3. Scroll down to Custom add-ins, click + Add a custom add-in, then Add from URL…
  4. Paste this address and click OK, then Install:
    manifest.xml
  5. Close Outlook completely and open it again. WriteFix now shows in every Outlook you sign in to.

If you can't see Custom add-ins, or you get an error saying add-ins aren't allowed, your organisation has turned this off. Send your IT admin the message below.

For your IT admin

In the Microsoft 365 admin center, go to Settings → Integrated apps → Upload custom apps. Choose Office Add-in, then Provide link to manifest file, paste the address above, and deploy it to the user. It can take up to 24 hours to appear. The add-in asks for ReadWriteItem only (the email being written). Email text goes only to the AI provider the user picks (Anthropic or OpenAI), straight from Outlook, using the user's own API key.

Use it

  1. Start a new email or a reply.
  2. Click WriteFix on the ribbon. In new Outlook it may be under the Apps button or the … menu.
  3. The first time, open Settings in the panel and paste your Claude API key.
  4. Click Fix spelling & grammar or Improve wording. Untick anything you don't want, then Apply. Undo puts the email back.
  5. Select some text first to check just that part. Quoted replies and your signature are always left alone.
  6. Click the pin in the panel's corner to keep it open as you move between emails.

Your key is kept in Outlook on each computer, so paste it once on each one.
